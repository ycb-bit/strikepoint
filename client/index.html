HOW TO PLAY

Movement

W A S D — move

Shift (hold) — sprint forward (drains stamina)

Ctrl / C (hold) — crouch (slower, more accurate)

Ctrl / C while sprinting — slide (speed burst!)

Space — jump

Combat

Mouse — aim · Left click — fire

Right click — ADS zoom (AWP gets a scope)

R — reload

1 / 2 / 3 — primary / pistol / knife

Tab — scoreboard

Modes

Defuse: T side plants the bomb (E in a site), CT side stops them or defuses (E on the bomb). Win 8 rounds. Buy gear with B during freeze.

TDM: first team to 40 kills. Respawn 3s. Pick your loadout any time (L).

FFA: everyone vs everyone — first to 30 kills. Loadout with (L).

Tips

Headshots deal big bonus damage — aim high!

Armor absorbs damage. Buy it (B) in defuse mode.

Sprinting hurts accuracy — stop or walk to shoot straight.
